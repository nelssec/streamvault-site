StreamVault

A player for the TV playlists you already have.

StreamVault plays IPTV playlists on iPhone and iPad. It comes with no channels or content of its own. You add a playlist you are licensed to use, either an Xtream Codes login or an M3U address, and optionally an XMLTV guide.

Getting started

  1. Open StreamVault and tap Add Playlist.
  2. Choose Xtream Codes and enter the server, username and password from your provider, or choose M3U and paste the playlist address.
  3. Tap Add. Channels, the guide, movies and shows load in a few seconds to a few minutes, depending on the size of the playlist.

Optional services

Some features use your own key for a third-party service. Add them in Settings → Keys for Optional Services.

  • TMDB adds posters, artwork and details for movies and shows.
  • OpenSubtitles finds subtitles (StreamVault Pro).
  • OpenAI or Anthropic powers Mia, the TV assistant (StreamVault Pro).

Keys are stored in your device's Keychain and are only sent to the service they belong to.

StreamVault Pro

A one-time purchase that adds more than one playlist, custom guide addresses, iCloud sync of My List, favorites and watch progress, Mia, subtitles, and two extra app icons. If you reinstall or get a new device, use Restore Purchases in Settings.

Common questions

A channel won't play. Check that it plays in your provider's own app. Some providers allow only one stream at a time, so stop playback on other devices. Some video formats (MKV, AVI) can't be played on iPhone yet.

The guide is empty or wrong. Not every playlist includes a guide. With Pro you can add a separate XMLTV guide address.

Where can I get a playlist? StreamVault doesn't provide or recommend any. Use a service you are licensed to use.
