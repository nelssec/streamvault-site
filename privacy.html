Privacy Policy

StreamVault for iPhone and iPad · Updated October 23, 2026

StreamVault has no accounts and no server of its own. We don't collect, store or sell your personal data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What stays on your device

Your playlists, login details, guide, watch history, favorites, My List and settings are stored on your device. Passwords and API keys are kept in the iOS Keychain.

Where the app connects

StreamVault only contacts services you set up yourself:

  • Your playlist and guide providers, to load channels and the guide and to play video. These requests go directly from your device to the addresses you entered.
  • TMDB, if you add a TMDB key: movie and show titles are sent to look up artwork and details.
  • OpenSubtitles, if you add a key: the title you're watching is sent to search for subtitles.
  • OpenAI or Anthropic, if you add a key and use Mia: your messages to Mia, and the catalog and guide details needed to answer them, are sent to the provider you chose. The app asks for your consent before the first message is sent.
  • YouTube, only when you tap a trailer, which opens in YouTube.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy. We never receive any of it.

Apple services

With StreamVault Pro, My List, favorites and watch progress can sync between your devices through your iCloud account. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't see your Apple ID or payment details.

Children

StreamVault is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a playlist in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device. Data synced through iCloud can be removed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: andrew@nelssec.com.
